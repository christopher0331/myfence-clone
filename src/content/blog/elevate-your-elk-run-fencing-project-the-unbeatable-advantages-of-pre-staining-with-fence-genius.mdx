### Why Traditional Staining Falls Short

Traditionally, fences are built and then stained on-site. While this method is common, it often comes with several drawbacks that can compromise the final result and the fence's long-term durability.

Factors like unpredictable weather, uneven application, and insufficient drying times can all lead to less-than-optimal protection. This can leave your new **fencing** vulnerable sooner than expected.

<Callout title="Real-world failure">
On-site staining can be highly susceptible to weather delays. A sudden rain shower can wash away freshly applied stain, leading to streaking, uneven coverage, and wasted materials and labor. This is particularly relevant in the **Washington fencing** climate.
</Callout>

## Introducing Pre-Staining: A Smarter Approach

**Pre-staining** is a revolutionary process where each piece of fence material – pickets, rails, and posts – is stained and sealed before it's assembled and installed. This takes place in a controlled environment, ensuring optimal conditions for stain application and curing.

This method guarantees that every surface, including hard-to-reach areas and end grains, receives a thorough and uniform coat of protective finish. It's a proactive step that significantly boosts the fence's resilience and aesthetic appeal.
