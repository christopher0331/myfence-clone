## Choosing the Right Fence Material for Seattle Homes

Selecting the right material is key to a long-lasting and beautiful fence. Here's a quick comparison of two popular options for **Seattle fence installation**:

| Feature | Western Red Cedar Fence | Vinyl (PVC) Fence |
|---------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| **Cost** | Moderate to High (varies with grade) | Moderate to High |
| **Durability** | Excellent natural resistance to rot, insects; lasts 15-20+ years with care | Extremely durable, resistant to rot, pests, fading; lasts 20-30+ years |
| **Maintenance** | Requires periodic staining/sealing to maintain appearance and prolong life | Low maintenance; occasional washing needed |
| **Aesthetics** | Natural, warm, classic wood look; customizable | Modern, clean look; available in various styles and colors |
| **PNW Suitability** | Highly suitable; naturally resistant to moisture, but benefits from sealing | Highly suitable; impervious to moisture and rot |
| **Privacy** | Excellent for solid panel designs | Excellent for solid panel designs |
