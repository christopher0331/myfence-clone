## The Foundation of Your Fence Investment: Why Pre-Stain?

**Fencing** is a significant investment that enhances your property's security, privacy, and curb appeal. Protecting that investment from the start is crucial, especially against the damp, moss-friendly weather patterns common in **Washington State**.

**Pre-staining** involves applying a protective stain and sealant to your fence boards *before* they are installed. This method ensures comprehensive coverage and superior protection compared to traditional post-installation staining.

<Callout title="Important" variant="info">
Pre-staining addresses common challenges posed by the Pacific Northwest climate, such as consistent moisture, UV exposure, and the growth of mold and mildew.
</Callout>
