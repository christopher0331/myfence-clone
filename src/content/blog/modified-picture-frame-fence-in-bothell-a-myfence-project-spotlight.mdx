## What is a Modified Picture Frame Fence?

A **Modified Picture Frame Fence** is an elegant variation of the traditional picture frame style. It’s characterized by its robust framework, where fence boards are installed within a sturdy frame, creating a clean, finished look on both sides of the fence.

Unlike a standard picture frame fence, the 'modified' aspect often involves design enhancements. This could include varied board widths, decorative top caps, or a combination of horizontal and vertical elements. The goal is to elevate the classic style with unique architectural details.

<Callout title="Pro tip" variant="success">
Opting for a **Modified Picture Frame Fence** ensures both you and your neighbors enjoy an attractive view. This symmetrical design is often highly favored by HOAs.
</Callout>

### Key Features of this Style:

-   **Dual-Sided Appeal**: Looks equally good from both sides, eliminating the 'good neighbor' side dilemma.
-   **Enhanced Privacy**: Boards are typically installed tightly, offering excellent visual privacy.
-   **Increased Durability**: The frame structure adds significant stability and longevity to the fence.
-   **Customization Potential**: Allows for unique design elements and material choices, making it a truly **Custom Fence Bothell** option.

### Navigating HOA Approved Fence Requirements

Homeowner Associations (HOAs) in Bothell often have strict guidelines regarding fence styles, materials, height, and setbacks. A key benefit of the **Modified Picture Frame Fence** is its high potential for being an **HOA Approved Fence**.

Its finished look on both sides and classic aesthetic generally align well with HOA standards. MyFence assists homeowners through the entire approval process, ensuring compliance from design to installation.

<Callout title="Important" variant="info">
Always check with your specific HOA before beginning any fence project. MyFence can help you review your HOA's covenants and restrictions.
</Callout>

### Typical HOA Fence Considerations:

1.  **Material Restrictions**: Some HOAs may specify wood type (e.g., cedar, redwood) or prohibit certain materials.
2.  **Height Limits**: Standard fence height limits are common, often 6 feet in backyards and 3-4 feet in front yards.
3.  **Color and Finish**: Specific stain colors or paint finishes might be required or disallowed.
4.  **Style Guidelines**: Certain fence styles, like chain link, might be prohibited, while others like picture frame or shadow box are preferred.
